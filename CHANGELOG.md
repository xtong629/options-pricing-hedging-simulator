# Release notes

## 1.0.0rc1 · Core Pricing, Risk & Hedging Analytics

The candidate packages analytical pricing, batched Monte Carlo, a self-financing short-call hedge and empirical risk analysis behind the optionlab CLI. Research commands support configurable sampling, isolated output directories and reproducible figures. Historical Python interfaces and example entry points remain available.
